---
name: edit-pdfs
description: Merge, reorder, rotate, extract, or watermark local PDF files using frogConvert.
---

Choose one frogConvert tool based on the user's requested operation:
- `pdf_merge` to concatenate two or more PDFs in order.
- `pdf_organize` to reorder, rotate, omit pages, or add blank pages.
- `pdf_extract` to split or extract specific 1-indexed pages.
- `pdf_watermark` to overlay a visible text watermark.

Use local paths for larger PDFs and distinct destinations for outputs. Confirm page-selection ambiguity only when necessary. A watermark is not encryption or access control. Preserve original PDFs. If the tool fails or is unavailable, do not claim the document was edited.
